# ADR-009: Cancellation, Return and Refund Policy

## Status
Accepted

## Date
2026-10-11

## Context
Under Section 2(46) of the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** (*Pioneer Urban Land (2019)*) and Section 74 of the **Indian Contract Act, 1872** (*Kailash Nath Associates (2015)*), blanket "no-refund under any circumstance" clauses and arbitrary forfeiture fees are deemed unfair contract terms and unlawful penalties. Simultaneously, Sri Ram & Co needs protection against fraudulent return requests or abuse on heavy machinery and power tools.

## Decision
Establish an objective, statutory-compliant Return, Cancellation, and Refund Policy:
1. **Cancellation Prior to Dispatch**:
   - Customers may cancel their order anytime prior to vehicle dispatch from the store hub with **100% full refund and zero cancellation fees** (complying with Section 74 ICA against penalty traps).
2. **7-Day Dead-On-Arrival (DOA) & Transit Damage Window**:
   - Items received in damaged condition, physically defective, or wrong SKU delivered are eligible for **100% free store replacement** or full refund when reported within 7 days of doorstep delivery.
3. **Refund Processing Turnaround (SLA)**:
   - For accepted returns, funds are credited back to the customer's original payment mode (UPI, NetBanking, Card) within **5 to 7 business days** of item inspection at the Trichy/Chennai store facility.
4. **Non-Returnable Exceptions**:
   - Consumables (cutting discs, oils, abrasives) once opened, or equipment exhibiting signs of electrical tampering, unauthorized repairs, or deliberate physical damage.

## Alternatives Considered
- **30-Day Unconditional Return Window**:
  - *Rejected*: Infeasible for high-value power tools and heavy appliances due to commercial "tool rental" abuse risks. A 7-day DOA/defect replacement window strikes the optimal legal balance.

## Consequences
- Complies with Consumer Protection Regulations while maintaining sustainable operational margins for heavy retail equipment.
