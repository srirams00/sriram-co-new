# ADR-007: Terms of Service and Conditions of Sale

## Status
Accepted

## Date
2026-10-11

## Context
Sri Ram & Co requires a legally binding Terms of Service (ToS) governing physical retail and direct e-commerce transactions across Tamil Nadu. The storefront sells consumer appliances, commercial cleaning tools, and high-voltage power tools. Under the **Indian Contract Act 1872** and **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020**, an e-commerce platform must specify offer-acceptance points, pricing, payment liabilities, product safety disclaimers, and dispute mechanisms without imposing unconscionable clauses (Sec 23) or unlawful litigation bars (Sec 28).

## Decision
Adopt a balanced, comprehensive Terms of Service architecture covering:
1. **Contract Formation & Acceptance**:
   - Order placement on the website constitutes an offer. Acceptance is finalized when the order is confirmed and dispatched from our Trichy or Chennai branch.
2. **Pricing, Invoicing & GST**:
   - All displayed prices include applicable CGST and SGST. Official GST tax invoices accompany every physical delivery.
3. **Power Tools & Appliance Safety Disclaimer**:
   - Electrical equipment and power tools must be operated strictly according to OEM instructions and safety guidelines. Sri Ram & Co limits consequential liability for improper usage, surge damage, or unauthorized modifications, in compliance with the *Sale of Goods Act 1930*.
4. **Governing Law & Local Jurisdiction**:
   - Non-predatory dispute clause anchoring jurisdiction in the competent courts of **Tiruchirappalli (Trichy) and Chennai, Tamil Nadu**, rejecting foreign arbitration traps (*Perkins Eastman (2020)* / *Food Corp of India (1994)*).
5. **Statutory Grievance Redressal**:
   - Explicit publication of the designated Grievance Officer (S. Ramkumar) with a statutory 48-hour acknowledgement SLA.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Standard Unilateral Boilerplate**:
  - *Rejected*: Common US/generic templates contain unilateral arbitration and absolute liability disclaimers, which are declared void under Section 2(46) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (*Pioneer Urban Land (2019)*).

## Consequences
- Protects Sri Ram & Co from liability resulting from industrial tool misuse while establishing an enforceable, fair commercial contract for buyers.
