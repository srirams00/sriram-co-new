# ADR-011: Warranty, Service Facilitation and Grievance Redressal Policy

## Status
Accepted

## Date
2026-10-11

## Context
Sri Ram & Co retails authentic brand-manufactured electrical appliances and tools (Bosch, Philips, Crompton, etc.). Misunderstanding often arises when consumers confuse retail store replacement obligations with long-term 1–3 year manufacturer warranties. Furthermore, Rule 4 of the **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020** mandates that every e-commerce platform maintain an accessible statutory Grievance Officer and nodal contact person.

## Decision
Establish a structured two-tiered warranty and statutory grievance architecture:
1. **Tier 1: Sri Ram & Co Store Replacement Guarantee (Days 1–7)**:
   - Covers manufacturing defects, Dead-On-Arrival units, and transit damage directly at the store level with 100% free product replacement.
2. **Tier 2: Brand Authorized Manufacturer Warranty (Post Day 7)**:
   - For the remaining warranty period (typically 6–36 months depending on the manufacturer), repairs and service are rendered by the brand's authorized service centers across Tamil Nadu.
   - Sri Ram & Co acts as customer advocate, providing duplicate GST invoices and facilitating service center bookings.
3. **Statutory Grievance Redressal Architecture**:

## Alternatives Considered
- **Outsourced Third-Party Call Center for Grievances**:
  - *Rejected*: Incompatible with Sri Ram & Co's personalized local service model. Direct management contact ensures compliance and customer retention.

## Consequences
- Full statutory compliance with the Ministry of Consumer Affairs guidelines.
- Clear distinction between store return responsibilities and OEM manufacturer obligations.
