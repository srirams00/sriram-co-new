# ADR-010: Shipping, Store Dispatch and Delivery Policy

## Status
Accepted

## Date
2026-10-11

## Context
Sri Ram & Co operates physical stores in Trichy and Chennai, utilizing a hybrid fulfillment model of direct store delivery personnel, counter pickup, and regional logistics couriers. Clear terms regarding dispatch timelines, recipient inspection, and title transfer are necessary under the **Sale of Goods Act, 1930** and the **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020**.

## Decision
Codify clear operational shipping and delivery guidelines:
1. **Fulfillment Origins**:
   - Deliveries originate directly from **Sri Ram & Co, No. 11 Vinola Complex, Trichy** and **Kothaval Chavadi Street, Chennai**.
2. **Delivery Timelines & Dispatch SLAs**:
   - **Local Metro (Trichy & Chennai)**: Same-day or next-day dispatch.
   - **Tamil Nadu State**: 24–48 hours delivery.
   - **South India Regional**: 2–4 business days via verified courier partners.
3. **In-Store Counter Pickup Option**:
   - Customers choosing "Store Counter Pickup" receive immediate notification when the parcel is packed and ready. Orders are held at the customer counter for up to 7 days upon presentation of the order confirmation SMS/email.
4. **Doorstep Inspection & Payment Verification**:
   - For door-step UPI and handheld POS card transactions, customers verify package exterior seal before payment completion.
5. **Failed Delivery Attempts**:
   - Store drivers attempt delivery twice. If the customer is unreachable or unavailable, the order is returned to the hub, and support contacts the buyer to reschedule.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Third-Party Dropshipping Model**:
  - *Rejected*: Sri Ram & Co maintains physical inventory and its own delivery drivers to ensure genuine quality and direct warranty service.

## Consequences
- Transparent delivery timelines reduce customer inquiries, provide clear SLAs, and establish physical custody transfer rules.
