## 3. SHIPPING CHARGES
- **Standard Store Delivery:** We provide **Free Store Delivery** for promotional e-commerce orders placed across core Tamil Nadu locations, as indicated during checkout.
- **Heavy Machinery & Outstation Cargo:** Certain bulky commercial equipment shipped outside primary delivery zones may carry nominal freight charges, clearly shown in your order summary before payment.

---

## 4. STORE COUNTER PICKUP (CLICK & COLLECT)
1. Customers may choose **"Store Counter Pickup"** during checkout at no extra charge.
2. Once your order is packaged and verified at our Trichy or Chennai branch, you will receive an SMS and email notification confirming pickup readiness.
3. Orders are held at the customer collection counter for up to **7 calendar days**. Please bring your order confirmation SMS and a valid photo ID.

---

## 5. DELIVERY VERIFICATION & PACKAGE HANDOVER
1. **Physical Inspection:** Our store driver or courier delivery partner will allow external carton verification upon arrival.
2. **Doorstep Payment:** For doorstep UPI or card orders, payment is completed after package inspection using the driver’s verified QR code or mobile POS device.
3. **Delivery Confirmation:** Deliveries are acknowledged via digital signature or one-time delivery verification code (OTP).

---

## 6. FAILED DELIVERY ATTEMPTS
1. Our delivery drivers attempt delivery up to two (2) times.
2. If the recipient is unreachable, our dispatch team will contact you via phone or WhatsApp to reschedule at a convenient time.
3. If delivery cannot be completed after 5 days, the parcel returns to our warehouse, and a full refund or re-dispatch is coordinated with the customer.
