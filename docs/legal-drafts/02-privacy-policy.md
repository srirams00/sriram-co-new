## 1. PURPOSE & COMMITMENT TO PRIVACY
Sri Ram & Co values customer trust. We act as a responsible Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is secured, and your statutory rights to access, rectify, and delete your information.

---

## 2. ITEMIZED DATA WE COLLECT
In accordance with statutory data minimization principles, we collect only the personal information strictly necessary to fulfill e-commerce services:
1. **Identity & Contact Details:** Full Name, Mobile Phone Number, and Optional Email Address (for electronic GST tax invoices and dispatch updates).
2. **Delivery & Address Data:** Physical Street Address, Landmark, City/Town, and Postal PIN Code (required for delivery routing and logistics).
3. **Authentication Data:** Firebase Authentication User ID (UID), hashed security tokens, and encrypted session cookies when you register or sign in via Google OAuth.
4. **Order History:** Records of purchased appliances, tools, order dates, payment status, and warranty invoice serial numbers.
5. **Technical Log Data:** IP address, browser type, and operating system logs recorded during web visits for fraud detection and system security.

*Note: We do NOT collect or store full credit card numbers, debit card PINs, or UPI PINs. Electronic card and UPI transactions are processed through RBI-authorized payment aggregators or handheld POS terminals.*

---

## 3. PURPOSE OF DATA PROCESSING (SECTION 6 DPDP ACT)
Your personal information is processed exclusively for specified lawful purposes:
- Coordinating door-step parcel delivery via our direct store drivers.
- Sending SMS and WhatsApp dispatch notifications, delivery OTPs, and payment confirmations.
- Issuing compliant GST tax invoices for tax and warranty verification.
- Providing manufacturer after-sales support and in-store service bookings.
- Preventing transaction fraud and unauthorized admin access.

---

## 4. STRICT PROHIBITION AGAINST DATA SALE
**We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize customer personal data.**  
Your phone number, address, and purchase records are never shared with third-party advertisers, telemarketing agencies, or data brokers.

---

## 5. DATA RETENTION & SECURITY SAFEGUARDS
1. **Retention Period:** Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill store delivery services and comply with statutory obligations under Indian tax laws (such as 6-year retention for GST transaction invoices).
2. **Security Controls:** All data in transit is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Database storage is protected by role-based access control, cryptographic salts, and strict firewall configurations.

---

## 6. YOUR STATUTORY RIGHTS (RIGHTS OF DATA PRINCIPALS)
Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the following enforceable rights:
1. **Right to Access & Summary:** Request a summary of the personal data Sri Ram & Co holds about you.
2. **Right to Correction & Erasure:** Request correction of inaccurate information or permanent deletion of your profile (subject to statutory GST record-keeping).
3. **Right to Grievance Redressal:** Lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer regarding data handling.
4. **Right to Withdraw Consent:** You may withdraw consent for promotional messages at any time.
